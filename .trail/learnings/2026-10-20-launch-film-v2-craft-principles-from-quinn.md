## Decided
- Added section "4. CRAFT PRINCIPLES" to prompts/marketing/app-launch-film-v2.txt, sourced from Apple's App Store app preview guidance (developer.apple.com/app-store/app-previews) plus coverage of Privacy, "Relax, it's iPhone", Big Fish (iPhone + Watch), and 2015 Watch spots.
- Wired into Phase 2.0 (PROPOSITION, viewer's moment, why-this-device moment, metaphor misreading notes, lockup candidates), muted-first hook, narration-free payoff hold, honest in-screen motion, gates G5.10 (muted) and G5.11 (craft).
- "Slogan" renamed to "lockup line" throughout.

## Tried and ruled out
- Copying Apple style literally: SF Pro is licensed only for Apple-platform mockups, and trade dress/tagline wording would break the prompt's own no-real-brands rule.

## Watch out
- Apple preview guidance says "no fingers tapping the screen"; the prompt keeps touch indicators (Apple permits touch hotspots) but not hands.
- Most ad analyses found were marketing blogs; only the Apple developer page is authoritative.
